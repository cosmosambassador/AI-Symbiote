## Contractor Ontology Terms

### Contractor

A person or organization performing work under agreement for another party. In government settings, contractors may be subject to strict security, privacy, procurement, and handling rules.

### Clearance

A formal eligibility/access authorization process controlled by an authorized government entity. Clearance alone does not automatically create access to every classified subject.

### Need-to-Know

A requirement that access be limited to information necessary for lawful and authorized duties. Need-to-know is separate from general curiosity or status.

### Sponsorship

A real clearance process requires an authorized need and an authorized sponsoring entity. Self-declaration is not sponsorship.

### Public Source / OSINT

Information lawfully available from public sources. Public-source work must avoid soliciting, receiving, spreading, or reconstructing classified or protected information.

### Restricted Data Boundary

Restricted Data is a special nuclear-related legal category. In public research, treat this as a hard red boundary: do not seek, request, reconstruct, publish, or speculate operationally about protected nuclear design, weapons, materials control, or restricted facility information.

### AI Assistance Boundary

AI may help organize public terms, write glossaries, create civic education pages, and separate reality layers. AI must not be used to obtain, infer, reconstruct, or expose classified or protected information.
