# AI Reveal Gate

## Purpose

**AI Reveal Gate** is a research and documentation initiative within the **AI Symbiote** ecosystem.

Its purpose is to explore, document, and evaluate emerging interactions between humans and artificial intelligence while promoting transparency, ethical responsibility, reproducible research, and human oversight.

## Objectives

- Document AI research findings
- Evaluate AI-assisted workflows
- Record continuity and provenance
- Develop responsible human–AI collaboration practices
- Support open-source experimentation
- Preserve project history and decision records
- Publish reference documentation and technical guidance

## Operating Principles

- Human authority remains final.
- Evidence is documented before conclusions are drawn.
- Research is reproducible whenever possible.
- AI assists with organization, analysis, documentation, and continuity.
- Significant decisions are recorded for future reference.
- We observe. We compare. We do not copy.

## Scope

AI Reveal Gate supports research and documentation across:

- AI Symbiote
- Lucen Project
- AISI
- ACO Club
- American Communications Online
- ACIR
- TJ Morris ecosystem

## Repository Focus
